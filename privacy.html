Privacy policy

Purrsuade · last updated 22 October 2026

Purrsuade is a speaking-practice game made by one person as a side project. This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps, where it goes, and how to delete it. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold.

Without an account

Everything (your cat, coins, journal, topics, transcripts and feedback) is saved only in this browser on this device. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it, so export a backup in Settings if you want to keep it.

With an account

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google or with your email, we keep:

  • Your sign-in: your email address (and, with Google, the basic profile Google shares: your name and email), stored by Amazon Cognito.
  • Your progress: one file with your cat, coins, levels, settings and journal, including the topics, transcripts, AI feedback and scores of your sessions. It's stored in Amazon Web Services (Sydney region) so you can restore it and use it on other devices. Earlier copies of this file are kept for up to 30 days in case something goes wrong.
  • A daily counter of how many instant feedbacks you've used, which deletes itself after a few days.

Your voice and transcripts

  • Recordings never leave your device. They are not uploaded or stored by us.
  • High-accuracy transcription runs on your device.
  • Quick transcription uses your browser's built-in speech service; some browsers (such as Chrome) send the audio to their maker (Google) to turn it into text.
  • Keyboard dictation uses your phone's keyboard, under your phone maker's terms.

AI feedback

Your talk only goes to an AI when you ask for feedback:

  • Instant feedback with your account: the topic, your transcript and your speaking time are sent to Claude (made by Anthropic) running in Amazon Bedrock on AWS. According to AWS, Bedrock doesn't use these requests to train models. We don't keep a separate copy; the feedback is saved in your progress like any other session.
  • Instant feedback with your own API key: your transcript goes from your browser straight to Anthropic, under your own Anthropic account and its terms. Your key is saved only on your device.
  • Your own AI app (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others): you send your talk yourself, under that app's terms.

Other services the app loads

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. The speech library and the AI library load from the jsDelivr network, and the speech model downloads once from Hugging Face, the first time they're needed. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your browser fetches files from them.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in and every saved copy of your progress from our storage straight away.
  • This device: Settings → Backup → Reset, or clear the site's data in your browser.

Children

Purrsuade isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

Questions and changes

Ask anything or request help with your data by opening an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
